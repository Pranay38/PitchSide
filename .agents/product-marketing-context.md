# The Touchline Dribble — shared marketing context

Approved 2 October 2026. Read with `docs/MARKETING-AND-GROWTH-STRATEGY.md`.
This context supersedes the Instigator Playbook and older launch/content-strategy brainstorms where they conflict. A user's explicit current direction takes precedence.

- **Audience:** Global English-speaking football fans who want a defensible opinion and a clearer understanding of the game.
- **Publication:** The Touchline Dribble. **Newsletter:** The Weekly Whistle.
- **Positioning:** Strong opinions on football’s biggest debates—with the evidence and knowledge behind them.
- **Newsletter promise:** One strong football opinion and one useful lesson, every week. A short welcome series introduces the writing first. Never promise a particular delivery day or early access without an actual schedule.
- **Voice:** Clear, opinionated, fair, curious. Challenge arguments with observable evidence. Include the strongest reasonable counterargument. Avoid insults, manufactured outrage, unsupported certainty and invented popularity claims.
- **Capacity:** One current opinion and one evergreen explainer weekly; one monthly story may replace an explainer. Marketing takes 3–5 hours weekly, excluding research/writing.
- **Channels:** Three text-first X posts weekly, useful participation in two suitable communities, website newsletter, and the required Substack companion. No Instagram, paid ads, bulk outreach or daily publishing quota in this phase.
- **Reader journey:** Discover → second article → optional subscription → return → one relevant existing participation feature. Preserve the match-minute homepage and existing access entitlements. Do not add features merely to fill the homepage.
- **Canonical content:** Website article is authoritative. Preserve citations and caveats when adapting it. Do not invent a public URL or factual claim. Apply `.agents/skills/blog-substack/SKILL.md` after publishable blog articles pass their delivery contract, including publication-intended rewrites, unless the user opts out.
- **Measurement:** Traffic source, second-article reading, genuinely new subscriptions, seven-day return rate, first meaningful participation. Existing-subscriber submissions are not new conversions. Use a reliable 28-day history or establish a two-week baseline; no invented counts or growth guarantees.
- **Execution boundary:** Drafting assets does not authorize publishing, sending emails, posting comments, contacting writers, buying ads or scheduling recurring actions.

For a promotion package use `.agents/skills/touchline-distribution/SKILL.md`. Use `docs/READER-GROWTH-OPERATIONS.md` for weekly practice, event definitions and rollout verification.
