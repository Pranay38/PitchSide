# Touchline distribution

Read `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` first and use the approved final article as the factual source. Inspect the article and its verified canonical URL. If either is missing, request that input; do not promote a draft as published or invent a destination.

Use existing `social-content` and `email-sequence` skills when their channel-specific guidance helps. Project context overrides generic daily posting quotas, channel defaults or SaaS advice. Do not require installing more skills.

Create a single `<article-slug>-distribution.md` beside the canonical source, or in `marketing/content/` when the source is remote. Include:

1. Three candidate openings; choose one and briefly explain its strongest supported argument.
2. Three self-contained X drafts: an argument with a reason, a concrete evidence example, and an explanation or fair counterargument. Use short individual posts unless the user requests a thread. Fit the current platform limit; verify platform rules when needed rather than guessing. These occupy the existing weekly slots, not three extra posts per article.
3. One community discussion draft containing a useful explanation and a question. Name a community only after checking relevance and its current rules. Disclose ownership where applicable; omit promotional links when not allowed. Do not select or contact communities automatically.
4. Weekly email material: subject, preview, a concise argument and a useful lesson drawn from the approved material, one primary article CTA and a reply question. If the article does not contain an explainer, suggest a verified existing explainer; do not fabricate one. This is material for the weekly edition, not another mandatory email.
5. A campaign-link table and a compact measurement note: what reader action this package should encourage, and what to review after seven days.

Use `utm_source=x`, the verified community slug, `weekly_whistle`, or `substack`; `utm_medium=social`, `community`, or `email`; `utm_campaign=<canonical-article-slug>`; `utm_content=argument`, `evidence`, `explainer`, `discussion`, or `weekly_edition`. Values use letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores only. Never put email addresses, account identifiers or private query strings into tracking links. If a URL is unknown, use `[FULL_ARTICLE_URL]` and flag links as unfinished.

Preserve nearby source links for factual claims carried into each asset. Clearly distinguish opinion from evidence. Do not invent scores, quotes, follower counts, research questions, testimonials or performance results. Flag claims needing verification rather than strengthening them.

Reuse the required Substack package if it exists. If this is part of a publishable `blog`, `blog-write`, or publication-intended `blog-rewrite` delivery, follow the project `blog-substack` skill after the canonical delivery contract passes; do not duplicate its work. A distribution-only request does not imply writing a new article or automatically triggering a Substack edition.

Before delivery, verify that all drafts fit the approved channels, workload and voice; links resolve to the approved article; and the Substack companion is present when required. Report unresolved source or URL gaps explicitly. Generating this package does not authorize posting, email sending, contact imports, outreach or scheduling.
