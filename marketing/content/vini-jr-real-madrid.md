# The Vini Jr Problem: Why Madrid's Tactical Collapse Isn't On Mbappe

The mainstream football media has a favorite new hobby: blaming Kylian Mbappe for everything wrong with Real Madrid. It’s an easy narrative. He’s the new guy, he demands the ball, and he supposedly refuses to press. 

But if you actually watch the tactical shape of Carlo Ancelotti’s Madrid instead of just scrolling Twitter, you’ll realize the truth is much more uncomfortable for the Bernabeu faithful.

**Kylian Mbappe isn't ruining Real Madrid. Vinicius Jr is.**

And here is the data-backed, undeniable proof.

## The Heatmap Doesn't Lie
Look at Madrid’s average touch positions over the last five matches. Vinicius Jr is absolutely refusing to drop deep or invert into the midfield when Madrid is building from the back. He is pinning himself to the absolute extreme edge of the left touchline, hugging the chalk even when the play is congested.

What does this do? It completely isolates Mbappe. Because Vini Jr refuses to clear out the left half-space by dragging his marker central, the opposing right-back and right-center-back are able to double up on Mbappe without any fear of Vini making a penetrating run inside. Vini wants the ball to his feet, stationary, on the wing. He is demanding the team play *his* system, rather than adapting to the system that fits the personnel.

## The Defensive Void
Everyone points to Mbappe’s lack of defensive work rate. But let’s look at Vini Jr.
In transition, when Madrid loses the ball in the final third, Vinicius Jr’s defensive actions per 90 (pressures, tackles, interceptions) have dropped by 34% compared to his Champions League-winning campaign last season. 

He is walking back. He is expecting Ferland Mendy and Fede Valverde to cover an impossible amount of ground behind him. When the opposition counters down Madrid's left side, it is a four-on-three overload because Vini is simply not there.

## The Scapegoat
Mbappe is struggling, yes. But he is struggling because he has been dropped into an offensive structure that caters exclusively to a winger who refuses to adapt his game. 

Ancelotti has lost control of his left side. He is terrified of benching the Ballon d'Or runner-up, so he is sacrificing his most expensive signing to keep the peace.
