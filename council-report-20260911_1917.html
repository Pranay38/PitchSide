Blind Spots the Council Caught

Through peer review, the council identified several critical blind spots in the initial advice:

  • The Cash Flow Panic: Every advisor failed to ask why you are suddenly abandoning your core "No AdSense" rule immediately after finishing a technical milestone. The peer reviewers correctly identified that this is likely a cash-flow panic driven by the development costs of "Platform Hardening," not a strategic pivot.
  • The "Subscriber" Assumption: All advisors assumed that your goal of 2,500 subscribers implies immediate paid monetization. None clarified whether you are aiming for a free email list or a paid tier, which drastically changes the immediate strategy.
  • The False Dichotomy: The advisors presented a false choice between "cheap AdSense" and "expensive subscriptions." They completely missed the middle ground: premium, curated ad networks (like Carbon Ads or BuySellAds) that actually align with high-end aesthetics, or leveraging your newly hardened platform for custom, native, non-intrusive sponsorships.
