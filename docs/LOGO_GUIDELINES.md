# The Touchline Dribble - Logo Guidelines

## 1. Logo Formats
- **Primary**: `public/logo.svg` (Always use the SVG format for web applications to ensure crisp scaling and small file size).
- **Fallback**: `public/logo.png` (Only use for systems that do not support SVG, such as specific email clients or older platforms).

## 2. Clear Space
To ensure the logo maintains its visual impact, always maintain a minimum clear space around it.
- **Rule**: Keep a padding equivalent to at least **25% of the logo's total height** on all four sides.
- No other graphic elements, typography, or borders should enter this space.

## 3. Sizing
- **Minimum Web Size**: Do not render the logo smaller than **32px** in height.
- **Favicon**: Use the dedicated `logo-small.png` or `logo-192.png` for favicons (which are optimized for smaller bounds).

## 4. Colors
The logo relies on the brand's primary colors:
- **Pitch Green**: `#16A34A` (Used for the accent bar and the word "DRIBBLE")
- **Premium White**: `#FAFAF8` (Used for "THE TOUCHLINE" in dark mode)
- **Deep Slate**: `#0A0A0A` (Used for "THE TOUCHLINE" in light mode, if applicable)

## 5. Misuse
To maintain brand integrity, avoid the following:
- ❌ Do not stretch or compress the logo disproportionately.
- ❌ Do not change the colors outside of the approved palette.
- ❌ Do not apply drop shadows or glow effects directly to the logo text (use CSS on the container if necessary, but keep the logo clean).
- ❌ Do not rotate the logo.
