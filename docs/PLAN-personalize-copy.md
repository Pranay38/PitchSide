# PLAN: Personalize Website Copy

## The Strategy: The "Opinionated Collective" Approach (Option A + C)

We are shifting the tone to be edgy, assertive, and highly opinionated ("us vs. the mainstream pundits"), while maintaining a collective "We" voice. We will not highlight specific author names (no "Pranay's Take"). The copy will cut through the noise and feel punchy, replacing boring publisher-speak with bold, direct language.

### Phase 1: Homepage Rewrite
- **Author's Take Banner:** "Author's Take" → "Our Weekly Verdict"
- **Calls to Action (CTAs):** 
  - "Read & Analyze" → "Hear our take"
  - "See every article" → "Read everything we wrote"
- **Section Headers:** 
  - *Quick Takes* → "Snap Judgments"
  - *Deep Reads* ("Big deeper reads.") → "The Deep Dive"
  - *The Wire* → "The Radar"
  - *Latest Analysis* ("Fresh from the feed.") → "Our Latest Takes"
  - *Editor Picks* ("Curated") → "Our Favorites" (subtext: "The pieces we're proud of.")
  - *From The Archive* ("More worth your time.") → "The Vault" (subtext: "Takes that aged perfectly.")

### Phase 2: About Page Refinement
- Maintain the edgy "Analysis your pundit won't give you" vibe, but shift it to a collective "We".
- Remove the individual bio ("Pranay Agrawal") and replace it with a collective "The Team" or "Us", focusing on our shared obsession with tactical truth over PR fluff.

### Phase 3: Footer & Microcopy
- **Newsletter CTA:** Make it punchier. e.g., "Let us send you our best work every week."
- **Empty States:** Shift from "No published content yet" to something more engaging.
